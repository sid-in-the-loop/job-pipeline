# Outreach templates

Rules: one specific verifiable detail per message (never invented); connection notes ≤280 chars; messages ≤120 words; one small ask; drafts only — the user sends; **always attach the resume** (the primary resume, `storage.resumes.primary`) to any DM or email that allows an attachment — it costs nothing and removes a round-trip, so never write "happy to send my resume" as if it were a later step; log every sent message to Outreach with Follow-up Due = +5 business days; exactly one follow-up, then close.

Fill `{school}`, `{program}` and `{grad}` from `policy.md` → Profile → "Outreach identity". Everything else in braces is per message.

**T1 Recruiter intro (post-apply):** Hi {Name} — I just applied to the {Role} role at {Company}. I'm a {program} student at {school} graduating {grad}, and {one line: most relevant experience matched to the role}. Wanted to introduce myself directly since I'd genuinely like to work on {specific team/product}. Resume attached; happy to share anything beyond it — thanks for your time!

**T2 Referral ask, alum:** Hi {Name} — fellow {school} alum-to-be here, graduating {grad}. I'm applying to {Role} at {Company}; it's near the top of my list because {specific reason}. {One line on most relevant thing built.} If you're comfortable, would you be open to referring me? Resume is attached and I can send the posting — totally understand if not. Either way, curious how you've found {Company}.

**T3 Referral ask, cold:** Hi {Name} — came across your work on {team/project} while researching {Company}. {school} {program}, graduating {grad}, applying to {Role}; {one line connecting experience to their team}. If you're open to it, I'd appreciate a referral — resume attached, and I'll send anything else that makes it easy. If referrals aren't your thing, no worries; I'd still value any advice on the team.

**T4 Follow-up (+5 business days):** Hi {Name} — following up on my note about {Role}. Still very interested in {Company}; since then {one new proof point}. Anything useful I can send over, say the word. Thanks!

**T5 Thank-you (post-OA/interview):** Hi {Name} — thanks for {the OA invite / your time today}. {One genuine specific.} Excited about the process — looking forward to next steps.
